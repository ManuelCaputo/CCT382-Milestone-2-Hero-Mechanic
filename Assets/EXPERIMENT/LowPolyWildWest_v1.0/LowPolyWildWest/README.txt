LOW POLY WILD WEST  v1.0
========================

127 game-ready low-poly models for building a frontier town: modular
storefronts and walls, boardwalks, horses, wagons, a stagecoach, railway
track with a timber bridge, saloon/sheriff/bank/store interiors, ranch
and mining props, and desert nature.
FREE / pay what you want.

GROUND (8)  - 2 x 2 m tiles, top surface at z=0 (boardwalk top at 0.3 m)
  Ground_Dirt, Ground_Road, Ground_Road_Tracks, Ground_Sand,
  Ground_Grass_Patchy, Boardwalk, Boardwalk_Steps, Floor_Wood

WALLS & STRUCTURE (20)  - walls 2 m wide, 3 m tall, street/room side faces -Y
  Plank walls: Wall_Plank, _Window, _Door, _SwingDoors, _Damaged, _Painted
  Log walls: Wall_Log, Wall_Log_Window
  Adobe walls: Wall_Adobe, Wall_Adobe_Window, Wall_Adobe_Door
  Wall_Corner_Post, Porch_Awning, Roof_Shingle, Roof_Tin, Roof_Flat
  False-front facades (4 m wide): Facade_Saloon, Facade_General_Store,
  Facade_Sheriff, Facade_Bank

BUILDINGS (5)
  Outhouse, Water_Tower, Windmill, Well, Stable_Stall

RAILWAY (10)  - rail tile is 4 x 4 m, all pieces have a gravel bed
  Rail_Track (2 m straight), Rail_Straight_4m, Rail_Corner (90 deg),
  Rail_Diagonal (corner-cut diagonal, chain these for diagonal lines),
  Rail_Diagonal_Join_A / _Join_B (diagonal that meets a straight at one end),
  Rail_Switch (straight with a diagonal branch and lever stand),
  Rail_Bridge_Start, Rail_Bridge_Middle (repeat for longer spans),
  Rail_Bridge_End  - timber-truss bridge, deck 2 m high
  Rotate pieces in 90 deg steps; every join lines up on the tile edges.

SPECS
  ~70,700 triangles total (about 560 per model on average)
  Real-world scale (metres)
  Floors: pivot at tile centre, top at z=0. Walls/facades: pivot at the base
  of the wall centre-line. Props: pivot bottom-centre.
  Hanging/wall items (Chandelier_Wheel, Wanted_Poster, Horns_Wall): pivot at
  the hook or wall.
  Jail_Cell has bars on the front and left side - place it in a room corner.
  Flat-shaded, no textures: shared solid-colour materials
  Lamps, lanterns and the campfire are emissive

FORMATS
  FBX/  - Unity, Unreal, Godot (scale applied, Y-up)
  GLB/  - Godot, Three.js, Babylon, web viewers
  OBJ/  - universal (with .mtl)
  LowPolyWildWest.blend - source (Blender 5.1) incl. the showcase town
